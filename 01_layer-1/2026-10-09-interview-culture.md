- Answer should be informative
- Czech and international engineering teams, colleagues are **comfortable** discussing disagreements openly.
- The important distinction is between challenging an idea and challenging someone's authority.
  - be prepared to defend your technical decisions without becoming argumentative.
  - a direct question or criticism doesn't necessarily mean the interviewer dislikes you.
- Understand what employers expect from someone with your experience such as:
  - JavaScript and Node.js fundamentals
  - Backend architecture
  - Databases
  - Testing and system design


|Situation|recommend approach|
|---|---|
|Introducing yourself|Give a focused 60-90 second introduction|
|Explain experience|Focus on what I personally implemented|
|Technical questions |Acknowledge uncertainty and explain your reasoning|
|Disagreeing|Disagree politely and support your opinion when needed|
|Asking question|Ask about architecture, team processes, and expectations|
|Discussing previos employer|Discuss technical challenges professionally|
|Interview punctuality|Be ready 5-10 minutes beforehand and test your equipment before start|
